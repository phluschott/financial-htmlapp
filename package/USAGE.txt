PAYCHECK PLANNER
================

A paycheck-by-paycheck planner. It runs entirely on your computer, in your
browser, with no internet connection and no account.

HOW TO START
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it (for example Documents).
  2. Open "index.html" in your browser (double-click it).
  3. Follow the short setup: your income, your bills and debts, any sinking funds.

SAVING YOUR DATA
  Chrome / Edge / Opera:
    Click "Choose app folder" and pick this same folder. The app then saves
    "finance-data.json" here automatically, and keeps timestamped backups in
    a "backups" folder. You may be asked to allow access each time you open it.
  Firefox / Safari / other browsers:
    These cannot save to a folder. Use "Save file" to download your data, and
    "Open a data file" to load it next time.

PASSWORD (OPTIONAL)
  Settings -> Set a password encrypts your data file (AES-256). There is no
  recovery: if you forget the password, the data cannot be opened.

KEEP IT PRIVATE
  "finance-data.json" and the "backups" folder contain your financial details.
  Do not share them or put them in a public place.

VERIFYING YOUR DOWNLOAD
  The download page lists a SHA-256 hash for the zip. Compare it with:
    Windows (PowerShell):  Get-FileHash .\<zipfile> -Algorithm SHA256
    macOS:                 shasum -a 256 <zipfile>
    Linux:                 sha256sum <zipfile>

This is a planning tool, not financial advice.
