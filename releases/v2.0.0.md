## Visual Paycheck Planner v2.0.0

> **1. It is only a visual aid.** It shows you where your money is going and when. That is all it is designed to do.
> **2. It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this app is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

### What's new
- **Balances page: is each card really going down?** Enter each credit card or loan's real balance (**Check in balances**) about once a month. For every card the app shows the change since the last check-in (down or up), what you paid split into minimum and extra, the estimated interest, and what is left over, labelled **charges / fees (estimate)**. A plain-words line sums it up, and warns when payments did not cover the interest.
- **Your numbers are the real ones.** Between check-ins the app only estimates a balance from your last actual, the APR, and the payments you recorded. Your next check-in replaces the estimate. Charges are not tracked.
- **APR on debts.** Set it in Setup (Edit a Debt payment item, or use the "add APR" link). It is used only for interest estimates.
- **Extra payment flag.** The Payments window and the Add dialog have a checkbox, "This is extra, on top of the minimum", so the Balances page can tell extra payments from the minimum.
- **Opening balance for sinking funds.** Enter what a fund already holds when you set it up (or edit it later). It sets the starting balance without coming out of any paycheck.
- The spreadsheet export has a new **Balance Check-ins** sheet.
- Not included on purpose: snowball/avalanche ordering and payoff projections, and spending tracking.

### Install
1. Download `visual-paycheck-planner-2.0.0.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Chrome / Edge autosave to a folder you choose; other browsers use **Save file** / **Open a data file**.

**Updating from an earlier version:** unzip over your existing folder (replace `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`). Your `finance-data.json` and `backups` folder are untouched, and your data opens unchanged. Old "Extra: ..." lines from the pre-1.0.3 extra payment button are not tied to a card, so the Balances page ignores them; re-add them with **Payments**.
