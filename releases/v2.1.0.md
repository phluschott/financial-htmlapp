## Visual Paycheck Planner v2.1.0

> **1. It is only a visual aid.** It shows you where your money is going and when. That is all it is designed to do.
> **2. It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this app is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

### What's new
- **A new home page, "Now".** It opens on today's date and the days until your next paycheck, with two boxes: how much is left to play with (or short) on **this paycheck** and on the **next paycheck**. Below them are the two paycheck cards, listing what is due in date order.
- **Subscriptions are hidden on the Now page.** They are still counted in every total, and a one-line note says what is hidden. Where a subscription is charged to a card, the card's line says "includes $X charged to this card". The **Month** page (the old Plan page) still shows every subscription.
- **Cards.** Your credit cards and loans appear on the Now page and on a **Cards** tab with balance, APR, payment and next due date, and the total owed. **Update balance** lets you type a balance by hand. It does not change your paycheck numbers. The month-to-month history is on the Cards tab, collapsed.
- The old Plan page is now called **Month**, and the old Balances page is now **Cards**.

### Fixes
- **An item starts at its first due date.** The date you enter on a recurring item is where it starts. Earlier repeats of the schedule are no longer created, so a bill you already paid before using the app (for example last month's Citi payment) does not show up. Anything you already ticked as paid or recorded a payment against stays.

### Install
1. Download `visual-paycheck-planner-2.1.0.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Chrome / Edge autosave to a folder you choose; other browsers use **Save file** / **Open a data file**.

**Updating from an earlier version:** unzip over your existing folder (replace `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`). Your `finance-data.json` and `backups` folder are untouched, and your data opens unchanged.
