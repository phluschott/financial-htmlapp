## Visual Paycheck Planner v1.0.2

> **1. It is only a visual aid.** It shows you where your money is going and when. That is all it is designed to do.
> **2. It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this app is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

### What's new
- **Next paycheck report.** A **Next paycheck report** button on the Plan page (and a **Report** link on every paycheck card) gives a plain-text summary: the income, every payment in date order with what is left after each, anything untracked, what needs confirming, whether you run short, and your subscriptions total for the month. Copy it to the clipboard or download it as a .txt file. If you paste it into an AI assistant, remember it contains your financial details.
- **Day-before reminder.** When your next paycheck lands today or tomorrow, a banner at the top of the Plan page offers its report.
- **Untracked items.** Mark a subscription or bill as *Untracked — paid from another account* (with an optional note naming the account). It still appears on the plan as a faded line and counts in the subscriptions box, but it is never deducted from your paycheck, "Going out," or the running balance. Changing an existing item applies going forward, so earlier months keep their numbers.

### Install
1. Download `visual-paycheck-planner-1.0.2.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Chrome / Edge autosave to a folder you choose; other browsers use **Save file** / **Open a data file**.

**Updating from an earlier version:** unzip over your existing folder (replace `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`). Your `finance-data.json` and `backups` folder are untouched, and your data opens unchanged.
