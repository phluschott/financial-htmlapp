## Visual Paycheck Planner v1.0.1

> **1. It is only a visual aid.** It shows you where your money is going and when. That is all it is designed to do.
> **2. It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this app is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

### What's new
- **Charged to.** Mark a subscription, bill, or other item as charged to a credit card or loan you track. It no longer comes out of the paycheck on its own: its amount is added to that debt's next payment, so it is never counted twice. The plan shows what a payment includes (for example "$168.00 payment + $16.00 Amazon Prime"), and a new **Charged to** column on the Setup page shows where each item is charged.
- **Subscriptions at a glance.** A fourth box on the month view totals the subscriptions due in the calendar month, including ones charged to a card.
- **New name:** Visual Paycheck Planner.
- The spreadsheet export has a new "Includes" column.

### Install
1. Download `visual-paycheck-planner-1.0.1.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Chrome / Edge autosave to a folder you choose; other browsers use **Save file** / **Open a data file**.

**Updating from v1.0.0:** unzip over your existing folder (replace `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`). Your `finance-data.json` and `backups` folder are untouched, and your data opens unchanged.
