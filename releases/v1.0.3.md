## Visual Paycheck Planner v1.0.3

> **1. It is only a visual aid.** It shows you where your money is going and when. That is all it is designed to do.
> **2. It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this app is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

### What's new
- **Payments nested under a debt.** A debt's due date now works like a small ledger. Use **Payments** on any debt line (or the **Add payment / allocation** button and choose "Payment toward a debt") to record a partial or extra payment. The payment is listed under the debt, the debt line shows what is still to pay (for example "$125.00 due, $40.00 paid, $85.00 to go"), and next month goes back to the usual amount.
- **The payment date picks the paycheck.** The money is deducted from the paycheck the payment came out of. A payment made today comes out of the paycheck for today's period, and the debt's own paycheck shows a note instead of deducting it twice. You can change which paycheck each payment came from, or remove it, in the Payments window.
- **Overpayments.** Paying more than is due is allowed and shown as "overpaid by $X". Paying in full checks the line as paid.
- You can also correct the amount due for one due date (for example when the real balance is lower than the usual minimum).
- If you already added an "Extra: ..." line with the old extra payment button, it stays as a separate line. Delete it and re-add the payment with **Payments** so the debt line reflects it.

### Install
1. Download `visual-paycheck-planner-1.0.3.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Chrome / Edge autosave to a folder you choose; other browsers use **Save file** / **Open a data file**.

**Updating from an earlier version:** unzip over your existing folder (replace `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`). Your `finance-data.json` and `backups` folder are untouched, and your data opens unchanged.
