## Paycheck Planner v1.0.0

First public release.

> **1. It is only a visual aid.** It shows you where your money is going and when. That is all it is designed to do.
> **2. It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this app is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

Paycheck Planner splits your month by paycheck. Each paycheck is a card listing everything that comes out before the next one, in date order, with a running balance and how much is left to play with.

### Highlights
- A card per paycheck, with your next paycheck highlighted, and a whole-month view
- Bills, subscriptions, credit card minimums, debts, and transfers assigned by due date (move or skip any one)
- Extra debt payments, transfers to savings, one-time bills, and sinking funds
- Paid and cleared checkboxes
- Export to spreadsheet (.xlsx)
- Runs offline in your browser; no account, no tracking, no network access
- Optional password protection (AES-256-GCM). There is no password recovery.

### Install
1. Download `paycheck-planner-1.0.0.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Chrome / Edge autosave to a folder you choose; other browsers use **Save file** / **Open a data file**.
