## Visual Paycheck Planner v2.0.1

> **1. It is only a visual aid.** It shows you where your money is going and when. That is all it is designed to do.
> **2. It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this app is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

### What's new
- **Balance adjustment.** A new choice in **Add payment / allocation**. It takes an amount (with an optional note, such as "groceries") off a paycheck's **Left to play with**, for spending or any correction. It is marked paid and can be edited or deleted like any other line. To add money back, use **+ Extra income**.

### Fixes
- **Extra payments now lower Left to play with.** A payment flagged "extra, on top of the minimum" used to reduce what was due and then be deducted on top, so the two cancelled out. It no longer reduces what is due, so it comes off Left to play with in full. A payment that is not flagged still counts toward what is due, so it does not change the number (it was already set aside). The Payments window explains the difference.
- **The date paid now picks the paycheck in the Payments window.** Changing **Date paid** switches **Paid from** to the paycheck that date falls in, as the Add dialog already did. A minimum paid last pay period now lowers that period's Left to play with, and raises the one where it was due.
- **Setup shows the real next due date** for each item. It used to show the stored schedule date, which looked stale (for example November dates in late November). Schedules themselves were always rolling forward correctly.
- **Moved lines are marked.** A bill moved to another paycheck shows "moved from <paycheck>" so a stray one is easy to spot.
- **Clearer Skip.** "Skip this time (already paid, not due, or handled elsewhere)" now says it is for bills you paid before you started using the app.

### Install
1. Download `visual-paycheck-planner-2.0.1.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Chrome / Edge autosave to a folder you choose; other browsers use **Save file** / **Open a data file**.

**Updating from an earlier version:** unzip over your existing folder (replace `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`). Your `finance-data.json` and `backups` folder are untouched, and your data opens unchanged. Payments you flagged "extra" before this version now lower Left to play with.
